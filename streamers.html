Tips

  • Once every 10 to 20 minutes is plenty for the big bar. The mini strip under your webcam can come more often, every 1 to 5 minutes.
  • The always-on badge stays put and only changes its line every few seconds, so it never gets in the way.
  • Make it yours: pick the colour, theme (dark, light or neon), corners, entrance and size above; the preview shows it right away.
  • Arabic and English viewers? Pick Arabic + English (alternating): it shows in Arabic, then in English next time, and so on.
  • Put the break screen in a scene of its own and switch to it when you step away.
  • Keep it clear of your webcam, chat and alerts. Pick the corner card if the bar covers your HUD.
  • Want it on demand? Choose Only when I show the source and toggle the source, or bind it to a hotkey.
  • The QR codes of the bar, card and mini strip scan from a phone at 720p and above; the badge’s smaller one is best at 1080p. Mention it on stream once in a while.
