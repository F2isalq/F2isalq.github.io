1Voice

Say “clip it”.

Hands busy? Say the phrase and QClips saves the replay. It catches it mid-sentence, like “nice, clip it”, and it understands Arabic as well as English.

  • Recognized on your PC. What the microphone hears never leaves your computer, and it works offline.
  • Your phrases, your lengths. Give each phrase its own length: 15 or 30 seconds, 1 to 2 minutes, or the whole replay.
  • Test it first. Start test shows what it heard and which phrase matched. The sensitivity slider sets how clear a phrase must be.
  • One download, once. The voice model comes from Settings when you turn voice on: about 67 MB for English, 164 MB for Arabic, 210 MB for both. The Arabic model is Powered by Moonshine AI.

2In the game

Or press Alt+Z without leaving the game.

A panel slides in from the edge of your main screen. Save the replay or just its end, start a recording, check your audio and open your latest clips.

  • Quick trim in the panel. Drag the two ends and save a trimmed copy without opening the editor.
  • Save just the end. The last 30 seconds, 1 minute or 2 minutes of the replay.
  • Your call what shows. Choose whether the panel, the “Clip saved” notice and the performance line appear in your clips and streams.
  • Hotkeys for everything. By default Alt+F10 saves the replay, Alt+F9 starts a recording and Alt+M switches the mic.

3Editor

Trim it to the moment. Size it for Discord.

Trim, split, change the speed (keeping the pitch), freeze a frame, add titles, effects, filters and transitions. Presets for Discord’s 10, 25 and 50 MB limits encode in two passes so the file fits.

  • Simple mode. Your library, text, effects, filters and transitions next to one timeline.
  • Advanced mode. Tracks, keyframes and masks. Animate position, scale, rotation, opacity and volume.
  • Titles in Arabic too. Titles join and read Arabic correctly, in the preview and in the exported file.

4Sound

Every sound on its own track.

Game, voice chat, microphone and any device each get their own track, plus a Mixed track for sharing. Friend too loud? Turn them down later in the editor.

  • Per-app sound. Put one program on its own track (Discord, Spotify, a browser), the current game, or everything except QClips.
  • No echo in Mixed. QClips warns when a program would be heard twice and keeps it out of the mix. A limiter keeps loud moments from crackling.
  • Without touching the game. Per-app audio never opens the game’s process.

Questions

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that normal?

Yes. The installers aren’t code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Download QClips only from this site or its GitHub releases page.

Where are my clips saved?

In your Videos folder, under Videos\QClips\Clips.

Settings, Storage shows how much space they take, lets you move every clip to another drive with its edits and favourites, and can set an optional size limit (the oldest clips go to the Recycle Bin; favourites are kept).

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it’s free, with no ads and no account. There is no cloud and nothing is uploaded: your clips stay on your PC until you share them.

Does QClips collect any data?

No. QClips sends no usage data, analytics or crash reports. It only goes online to check for updates and, if you turn on voice commands, to download the voice model once.

How big are the voice models?

They download once, only if you turn voice commands on: about 67 MB for English, about 164 MB for Arabic, or about 210 MB for both. After that, voice works offline.

Can it get me banned in games with anti-cheat?

QClips doesn’t interact with games at all: no injection, no hooks, it never opens the game’s process and it needs no administrator rights. It captures the screen and sound through Windows, like the built-in Windows tools.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, or Windows 11, 64-bit. A graphics card with a video encoder (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF or Intel Quick Sync) is recommended; without one, QClips records with the CPU. The app takes about 250 MB, plus room for your clips.

Is QClips available in Arabic?

Voice commands understand Arabic and English, and titles in the editor join and read Arabic correctly. This site is in Arabic too.
