Questions

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is that normal?

Yes. The installers aren’t code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns the first time. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Download QClips only from this site or its GitHub releases page.

Where are my clips saved?

In your Videos folder, under Videos\QClips\Clips, in a folder for each game or app. To keep them all in one folder instead, pick “All clips in one folder” in Settings, Storage (the game stays in the file name).

Settings, Storage shows how much space they take, lets you move every clip to another drive with its edits and favourites, and can set an optional size limit (the oldest clips go to the Recycle Bin; favourites are kept).

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it’s free, with no ads and no account. There is no cloud and nothing is uploaded: your clips stay on your PC until you share them.

Does QClips collect any data?

No. QClips sends no usage data, analytics or crash reports. It only goes online to check for updates, to download the captions models once if you use them, and when you share a clip to a site yourself.

How big are the voice models?

About 200 MB for English and Arabic together. They come with QClips when you install it, so there is nothing to download yourself, and voice works offline.

Can it get me banned in games with anti-cheat?

QClips doesn’t interact with games at all: no injection, no hooks, it never opens the game’s process and it needs no administrator rights. It records the screen with Windows Graphics Capture, the way Windows’ own tools do, and the sound through Windows’ audio system.

NVIDIA’s app uses Alt+Z and Alt+F10 too. What if I have both?

Change the keys in one of them. In QClips, open Settings, Hotkeys (or the hotkeys in the Alt+Z panel), click a shortcut and press the new keys. Backspace clears one, and Restore defaults puts them all back.

Why did my VALORANT clips show the desktop?

Many screen recorders, and QClips before 2.1, capture the screen with Desktop Duplication, all the time. After a while of that, VALORANT’s anti-cheat hides the game’s window from every screen capture until the PC restarts, so clips show what is behind the game, usually the desktop.

QClips 2.1 records with Windows Graphics Capture instead, so this no longer happens. If it already happened before you updated, restart your PC once.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 version 2004 (build 19041) or later, or Windows 11, 64-bit. A graphics card with a video encoder (NVIDIA NVENC, AMD AMF or Intel Quick Sync) is recommended; without one, QClips records with the CPU. The app takes about 250 MB, plus room for your clips.

Is QClips available in Arabic?

Yes. The whole app, the Alt+Z panel and the installer are in Arabic, right to left. QClips follows your Windows display language; to choose yourself, go to Settings, General, Language. Voice commands and captions understand Arabic and English. This site is in Arabic too.
